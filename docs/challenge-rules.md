## Questions the panel said it will ask (from the orientation slides)

The paper should answer each of these in plain words. Tick when a section covers it.

- [ ] Is the problem real and socially useful? (evidence: statistics, interviews, literature)
- [ ] Why is AI necessary?
- [ ] Are the scope and success criteria clearly defined?
- [ ] Is the dataset legitimate, sufficient and ethically usable? (source, consent, cleaning, bias check, split, documentation)
- [ ] Is there a baseline and a valid evaluation strategy? (regression: MAE, RMSE; system: reliability, usability)
- [ ] Can we reproduce and explain the results?
- [ ] Is the complete system usable, secure and maintainable? (UI, API, model, storage, deployment)
- [ ] Are generative AI tools and outside resources disclosed?
- [ ] Is the claimed contribution our own work?
- [ ] Are limits, risks and future work discussed openly?
